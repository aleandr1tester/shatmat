Background music for ShatMat.

The file theme.wav plays in a loop on a separate channel: short move,
capture and twilight sounds do not stop it.

The theme was written for the project (synthesised, no third-party samples),
so it is distributed under the project licence. You can use your own:
replace theme.wav with a WAV file (16-bit PCM, 22050 Hz or 44100 Hz, mono or
stereo) and restart the game.

Music can be enabled or disabled in the settings.
