Background music for ModeShift: Bipolar.

The file theme.wav plays in a loop on a separate channel: short move,
capture and twilight sounds do not stop it.

You can replace theme.wav with your own WAV file
(16-bit PCM, 22050 Hz or 44100 Hz, mono or stereo).
After replacing it, restart the game.

Music can be enabled or disabled in the settings.
